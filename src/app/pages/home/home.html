Our story

From farm waste to your table

Across Tamil Nadu, banana stems, sugarcane bagasse, and rice husk are routinely burned after harvest — one of the largest sources of agricultural air pollution in the region. We upcycle those by-products into tableware that replaces single-use plastic and paper plates.

The materials are stronger than they look, food-safe by virtue of being chemical-free, and biodegrade within weeks of being discarded. We close the loop, support the circular economy, and give farmers a second revenue stream from what they used to set on fire.

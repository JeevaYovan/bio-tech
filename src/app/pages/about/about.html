About

We turn farm waste
into tableware.

Rathika Biotech Products is based in Neelambur, on the Avinashi Road outskirts of Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. We manufacture biodegradable tableware from agricultural by-products that would otherwise be burned at the end of each harvest season.

A simple loop, run honestly

The materials are local and unmodified. Banana fiber comes from the stems of the banana plant after the fruit is harvested. Sugarcane bagasse is the dry, fibrous residue left after the juice is pressed out. Rice husk is the outer shell of the rice grain, removed during milling.

Across Tamil Nadu, all three are routinely set on fire because there is no profitable secondary use, and the resulting smoke is one of the largest contributors to seasonal air pollution in the region. We buy these by-products at a small but steady rate, press them into food-safe forms with a plant-based binder, and sell the result back into the same restaurants, weddings, and households that would otherwise be using single-use plastic or paper.

Three things we are careful about: the products must be strong enough to hold hot food without leaking, they must contain no synthetic coatings or adhesives so they can biodegrade and compost cleanly, and they must be priced low enough that switching from plastic is not a sacrifice.

Inputs

Three materials, all from the field

  • i.

    Banana fiber

    Extracted from the pseudo-stem of the banana plant after the fruit is cut. Naturally tensile, smooth-finished, and the binding agent in most of our presses.

  • ii.

    Sugarcane bagasse

    The fibrous residue from sugar mills after juice extraction. Light, insulating, and gives our cups, bowls, and parcel boxes their structural body.

  • iii.

    Rice husk

    The protective outer hull of the rice grain, separated during milling. Adds rigidity to plates and partition trays without making them brittle.

Hand-pressed at Neelambur

The production is small. We hand-press most pieces at the Neelambur workshop, which means tone and texture vary slightly between batches — the bagasse-husk-fiber mix has natural variability and we don't try to hide it. Every product carries a subtle Rathika emboss on the base.

Discarded outdoors, our products biodegrade within four to six weeks; in soil, within three to five weeks. They are fully compostable in a home compost system. Animals can safely consume them. There is no plastic or chemical residue at any stage of disposal.

Who orders from us

Restaurants, weddings, households

We supply restaurants, cafés, food trucks, wedding caterers, festival vendors, fruit shops, organic grocery stores, eco-friendly markets, and individual households. Bulk pricing for cases of 500 or more units is available; the smallest order is one carton.

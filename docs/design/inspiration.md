# Inspiration

References AND anti-references. Anti-references are equally important
— they tell `@klaro-curator` what NOT to ship.

## References (yes — capture this feel)

_Add specific URLs as the visual direction firms up. For an editorial
small-business product site with an honest/tactile feel, candidates to
study include:_

- Editorial product brands with strong photography and restrained
  typography
- Small-batch food/material brands (think: olive oil, ceramics,
  textiles) — they get the honest/tactile tone right
- Indian craft / heritage brands that handle bilingual elegantly

(User to add: links to specific sites whose hero, product page, or
story page should inform the bio-tech equivalent.)

## Anti-references (no — avoid this feel)

- Generic e-commerce templates (Shopify defaults, dawn theme look)
- "Eco" sites that lean on stock greenery, gradient hero, and recycled-
  cardboard textures used as decoration rather than substance
- Corporate "sustainability" pages with abstract glass globe imagery
- AI-generated illustrations standing in for real product photography
- Rounded-2xl-everything modern startup look ("shadcn-default")

## Photography direction
Real products on real surfaces (wood, stone, fabric). Natural light
preferred over studio. The textures of bagasse and natural fibers are
the brand's strongest visual asset — show them close.
